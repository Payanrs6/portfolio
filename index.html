About me

I'm Rayane Ould Ali.

My journey started in Michelet, Tizi Ouzou, before taking me to Bir El Djir, Oran, Algeria, where I continued building my path in technology.

Today, I'm a Master's student in Data Science at the University of Science and Technology of Oran – Mohamed Boudiaf (USTO-MB).

But my journey in technology is about more than a degree.

I've always been drawn to building things, solving problems, learning how systems work, and turning ideas into something real. From software development and mobile applications to backend systems and cybersecurity, every project has been another step in understanding what I can create.

I'm still figuring out exactly where this journey will take me.

But one thing is certain:

I'm not here just to follow a path.
I'm here to build my own.
